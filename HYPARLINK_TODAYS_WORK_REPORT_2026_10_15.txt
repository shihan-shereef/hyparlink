Today, October 15, 2026, the Hyparlink dashboard and video-generation engine underwent

stiff and robotic presentations, added intelligent AI background music synchronization,
infused the Antigravity 3-layer motion design system, and built an end-to-end Intelligent

Users can now enter natural language prompts (e.g. "Create a 60-second cinematic
advertisement explaining the history of Coca-Cola, from its creation in 1886 to its
worldwide popularity today") and the system will autonomously:

  3. Search legitimate open and stock sources (Wikimedia Commons, Pexels, Unsplash)

  4. Evaluate and rank candidate images using a 10-factor multi-attribute algorithm.
  5. Enforce strict copyright and commercial licensing verification.
  6. Safely download and verify assets using binary magic-byte format inspection.
  7. Bind images into HyperFrames scene files with cinematic GSAP camera pushes,
     slow parallax reveals, and kinetic typography.
  8. Intelligently select and duck background music for vocal clarity.
  9. Render a broadcast-quality 1080p MP4 validated through FFmpeg and FFprobe.
  10. Display full asset provenance and licensing attributions in the dashboard.

3. BACKEND & AGENTIC ARCHITECTURAL UPGRADES
================================================================================

A. MULTI-PROVIDER OPEN-WEB IMAGE DISCOVERY ENGINE
   - `ai-composition-generator/src/images/types.ts`: Standardized interfaces for
     candidates, licenses, scene requirements, and attribution reports.
   - `ai-composition-generator/src/images/wikimedia-provider.ts`: Official MediaWiki
     API search parser extracting image URLs, dimensions, and license metadata.
     Includes automated keyword simplification fallback for zero-result queries.
   - `ai-composition-generator/src/images/pexels-provider.ts`: Pexels stock photography API.
   - `ai-composition-generator/src/images/unsplash-provider.ts`: Unsplash photo API.
   - `ai-composition-generator/src/images/workspace-provider.ts`: Local user workspace
     asset scanner guaranteeing user assets take absolute priority (#1).

B. 10-FACTOR MULTI-ATTRIBUTE IMAGE RANKER (`image-ranker.ts`)
   - Replaces naive first-result selection with a weighted multi-attribute algorithm:
     1. Subject Relevance (22%)
     2. Scene Description Match (18%)
     3. Resolution Quality (15%)
     4. Aspect Ratio Alignment (10%)
     5. Historical / Factual Accuracy (10%)
     6. Visual Quality (8%)
     7. Animation & Cropping Suitability (7%)
     8. Composition & Negative Space (5%)
     9. Licensing Compliance (5% gating)
     10. Source Reliability (5%)

C. SAFE IMAGE DOWNLOADER WITH MAGIC-BYTE INSPECTION (`image-downloader.ts`)
   - SSRF Protection: Blocks loopback, private subnets (10.x, 192.168.x, 172.16.x),
     and AWS/GCP instance metadata IP addresses.
   - Binary Magic-Byte Inspection: Inspects initial file bytes before saving:
     * JPEG: 0xFF 0xD8 0xFF
     * PNG:  0x89 0x50 0x4E 0x47
     * WebP: 0x52 0x49 0x46 0x46 ... 0x57 0x45 0x42 0x50
   - File Size Guardrails: Rejects corrupt error pages (< 5 KB) or oversized files (> 30 MB).
   - MD5 Cache: Caches downloaded assets to prevent duplicate network requests.

D. ANTIGRAVITY MOTION DESIGN SYSTEM INFUSION (`composition-synthesizer.ts`)
   - Injected 3 distinct depth planes:
     * Layer 0 (Atmosphere): Micro-dot matrix grid (36px x 36px) with dual drifting
       Gaussian blur orbs (blur: 95px, 105px) and radial vignette masks.
     * Layer 1 (Midground): Hardware-accelerated glassmorphic HUD telemetry cards
       (backdrop-filter: blur(28px), 1px solid borders, pulsing green status dots).
     * Layer 2 (Foreground): High-contrast kinetic typography with background-clip
       gradients (56px–72px display headlines vs. 13px monospace sub-tags).
   - Camera Physics: Continuous 3.5% camera push (`scale: 1.035`) across every scene.
   - Dynamic Eases: Steep exponential eases (`power4.out`, `expo.out`, `back.out(1.6)`)
     with staggered 0.08s entrances.

E. INTELLIGENT SOUNDTRACK SELECTION & AUDIO MIXING
   - `music-intelligence.ts`: Analyzes script narrative, pacing, and tone to generate
     a tailored music brief.
   - `music-ranker.ts`: Matches brief against curated audio library.
   - `audio-mixer.ts`: Uses FFmpeg to apply sidechain audio ducking (lowering music
     volume by -14dB to -18dB whenever speech or avatar voice is active, restoring
     music during pauses and scene transitions).

F. CLOSED-LOOP QUALITY GATE & AUTO-HEALING
   - Pre-Render Linter Healer: Auto-fixes HyperFrames rules (tag mismatches, missing
     data-start attributes, duplicate media discovery, non-deterministic timers).
   - Post-Render FFprobe & Keyframe Inspector: Validates MP4 streams and extracts
     sample frames to ensure no blank or black screens exist before completing jobs.
