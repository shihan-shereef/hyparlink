2. FORENSIC ROOT-CAUSE ANALYSIS: DASHBOARD VS. ANTIGRAVITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When the user entered the prompt:
"Create a 30-second cinematic DC superman ad for clurk Kent with voice and logo of the
superman and presentation of Clark Kent"

The dashboard originally produced a 1.3 MB video with corporate voiceover and plain text.
Forensic investigation revealed three architectural root causes:

A. Hardcoded Fallback Voiceover in backend-echo/server.mjs:
   - Line 1952 checked `if (isGL)` (Green Lantern).
   - Any other prompt evaluated to false and fell into a generic corporate array:
     "Experience the next level of cinematic motion graphics... Engineered with precision timing..."
   - No character-specific dialogue was written or synthesized for Superman.

B. Zero Image Generation in the Dashboard's Text LLM Connection:
   - The dashboard connects to Ollama (api.ollima.com), which only produces JSON text briefs.
   - Ollama cannot create images.
   - For Green Lantern, Antigravity previously generated and placed assets in presets/gl-assets/.
   - For Superman, `assets` was completely empty (`[]`).
   - Without images, the engine was forced to render plain text on blank CSS containers.

C. Missing Bespoke Scene Layouts in ScenePlanner:
   - Bespoke layouts only existed for Green Lantern, Batman, and standard charts.
   - Superman defaulted to generic headline-body cards.
