Bug #1 – Whiteboard Options and Annotation Controls Not Fully Visible
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Module: Whiteboard / Shared Board
Severity: High (UI Clipping / Functional Inaccessibility)

Issue:
When opening the Whiteboard and clicking on Board Options, the options such as 
Edit Shared Board, Pause Share, and Change are not fully visible within the window.

Additionally, under the options menu, the Annotation Requests section displays:
- Malavika asks to annotate this source.
- Approve button is visible.
- Deny button is positioned outside the visible window and cannot be accessed.

Steps to Reproduce:
1. Join an instant meeting.
2. Open the Whiteboard.
3. Click on Board Options.
4. Check the visibility of Edit Shared Board, Pause Share and Change.
5. Scroll down to Annotation Requests.
6. Check whether both Approve and Deny buttons are accessible.

- All Board Options should be fully visible within the available window.

Impact:
Users are unable to deny annotation requests or access primary whiteboard management actions due to UI boundaries clipping critical controls.

Bug #2 – Cloud Recording Includes the Recording Paused Screen
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Module: Ollasync – Cloud Recording
Severity: High (Video Output Quality & User Experience)

Issue:
When using cloud recording, if the recording is paused for a break and resumed 
after approximately 30 seconds, the saved video includes the entire paused period.

For example, when recording is paused at the 3-minute mark and resumed after 30 seconds, 
the saved video displays the "Recording paused – The class continues after the break" 
screen for those 30 seconds before continuing with the actual meeting recording.

Steps to Reproduce:
1. Start a cloud recording during an instant meeting.
2. Allow the recording to continue for approximately 3 minutes.
3. Pause the recording.
4. Wait for approximately 30 seconds.
5. Resume the recording.
6. Stop and save the recording.
7. Open the saved video and check the playback around the pause and resume point.

Expected Result:
The saved recording should exclude the paused interval. When the recording resumes, 
the video should continue directly from the point where it was paused, without 
including the 30-second paused screen.

Actual Result:
The saved video includes the 30-second paused screen with the message:
"Recording paused – The class continues after the break."
The actual meeting recording resumes only after this screen has played.

Impact:
Unnecessary paused-screen footage is included in the final cloud recording, 
affecting the viewing experience and making the saved recording longer than the 
actual recorded meeting content.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Bug #3 – Cloud Recording Timer Badge Continues During Pause Without Freezing
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Module: Ollasync – Cloud Recording Timer & Badge
Severity: Medium (UI Timing Inaccuracy)

Issue:
When recording is paused (e.g., at 03:00 minutes) and kept paused for 30 seconds, 
upon unpausing the timer should resume from 03:00. Instead, it immediately jumps to 
and resumes from 03:30, counting the paused duration as active recording time.

Steps to Reproduce:
1. Start recording in an instant meeting.
2. Wait until the timer reaches 03:00 (3 minutes).
3. Click "Pause Recording".
4. Wait for 30 seconds in the paused state.
5. Click "Resume Recording".
6. Observe the timer badge.

Expected Result:
The timer badge must pause at 03:00 and continue from 03:00 upon resuming.

Actual Result:
The timer continues accumulating elapsed wall-clock time in the background. 
When resumed at 30 seconds, the timer displays 03:30 instead of 03:00.

Impact:
The user is misled regarding the actual length of recorded footage.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Bug #4 – Cloud Recording Stop & Restart Timer Drift (Failure to Reset to 00:00)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Module: Ollasync – Cloud Recording Session Manager
Severity: High (Session State Bleed)

Issue:
After stopping a cloud recording at 04:00 (4 minutes), waiting 30 seconds, and 
starting a brand new recording session, the timer badge starts displaying 04:30 
instead of resetting and starting cleanly from 00:00.

Steps to Reproduce:
1. Start a cloud recording session.
2. Record until 04:00 (4 minutes).
3. Click "Stop Recording".
4. Wait 30 seconds.
5. Click "Start Recording" to start a new recording session.
6. Observe the initial timer value.

Expected Result:
Stopping a session must completely tear down and reset timer state. A new recording 
must begin at 00:00.

Actual Result:
The timer badge shows 04:30 (previous 4 minutes + 30-second gap) instead of 00:00.

Impact:
Multiple recordings taken in the same meeting session display corrupted timestamps 
and duration metrics.
