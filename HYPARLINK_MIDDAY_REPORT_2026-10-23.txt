DETAILED BREAKDOWN OF PROBLEMS RESOLVED & DELIVERABLES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. PREVIEW PLAYER SYNCHRONIZATION WITH CUTS & CUT GAPS (FIXED)
   - Issue:
     When users split a video clip into three parts on the timeline and deleted
     the middle segment, the clip was removed from the timeline, but the canvas
     video preview continued to play linearly through the deleted segment as if
     no edit had occurred.
   - Root Cause:
     The preview video player was bound directly to linear timeline time (0 -> duration)
     rather than computing non-linear media slices. It lacked media offset tracking
     and cut gap detection.
   - Resolution:
     * Implemented a professional NLE (Non-Linear Editing) media sync engine in
       apps/app/src/components/native-editor/interactive-canvas.tsx.
     * Added explicit `mediaOffset` mapping to every scene clip in timeline-utils.ts
       and native-editor-view.tsx, ensuring that splits preserve accurate source
       in/out time offsets.
     * Added real-time playhead gap detection: when the playhead enters a cut gap
       between remaining clips, the video pauses during scrubbing and automatically
       jumps over the gap during active playback to the start of the next valid clip.
     * Added interactive "Gap" visual chips on the timeline with 1-click "Close Gap"
       (Ripple Close) functionality.

2. VIDEO PLAYBACK SPEED MULTIPLIER CONTROLS (IMPLEMENTED)

3. SIDEBAR NAVIGATION & STACKING CONTEXT FROM NATIVE EDITOR (FIXED)

     (Create Video, Live Editor, Projects, Assets, Trash, + Create) or the back
     button failed to navigate or froze the interface.
   - Root Cause:
     1. Stacking context: Canvas overlays and header containers were intercepting

   - Resolution:
     * Updated apps/app/src/app/[locale]/(dashboard)/_components/sidebar.tsx:
       - Elevated sidebar stacking context to `relative z-40`.
       - Implemented `handleEditorNav`: detects when navigating away from /editor/

     * Updated the `<- Projects` back link in editor/[projectId]/page.tsx with a
       clean event handler to ensure immediate exit back to the project list.
